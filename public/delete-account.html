Fussky

Delete your Fussky account

Fussky is a shared-expense app published on Google Play as Fussky (com.fussky.app).

Option 1: delete it in the app

  1. Open Fussky and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Option 2: ask us by email

If you no longer have the app, email chiragguptainvestment@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Fussky account", sent from the Google account you signed in with.

We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in identity (your Google login to Fussky)
  • Your profile: name, profile photo, UPI ID, currency, budgets and preferences
  • Your personal (non-group) expenses
  • Your notifications

What is kept, and why

Expenses you shared in a group also belong to the other people in it, so they stay in that group to keep everyone's balances correct. Your name in those groups is marked "(Left)", and your email stays attached to those past entries so the history stays consistent (and so you can pick them up again if you rejoin).

To have your email removed from those shared entries too, say so in your email and we'll replace it with an anonymous label within 30 days.
