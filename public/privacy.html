Fussky

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026. Applies to the Fussky app on Android (com.fussky.app) and the website at fussky.com.

Fussky helps friends and groups split shared expenses. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. We don't show ads and we don't sell your data.

What we collect

  • Account details from Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile photo.
  • Profile settings you enter: display name, photo, UPI ID, default currency, budgets and app preferences.
  • Expense data you enter: groups, the names and email addresses of people you add, and expenses (description, amount, date, who paid and how it was split).
  • Notifications about activity in your groups.
  • Data you import from Splitwise, only if you choose to import it.
  • Crash reports: technical error details when the app crashes. These don't include your name, email, amounts or IP address.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your data in sync across your devices.
  • To show shared expenses and balances to the other members of your groups. People in a group can see that group's expenses and member names.
  • To show your UPI ID to people who owe you money, so they can pay you.
  • To find and fix crashes.

Who processes it

We use a few service providers to run Fussky. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database and sign-in
  • Google: sign-in with your Google account
  • Vercel: hosting for the website
  • Sentry: crash reports

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Some data is also stored on your device so the app works offline.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your sign-in identity, profile, personal expenses and notifications are deleted. Expenses shared in a group stay with that group so other members' balances stay correct; see Delete your account for details.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile and expenses at any time in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete Account) or by email; see Delete your account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, by email.

Children

Fussky is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its effective date.
